Securely Hello ("the app", "we") is operated by [operator name, address, contact e-mail]. This policy explains what the app and its server process, and why. The short version: we cannot read your messages, calls or files.

What never leaves your device

  • The text of your messages, your calls, and the files you send. They are encrypted on your device before they leave it and can only be decrypted on the devices of the people you are talking to (and your own other devices).
  • Your private keys and your chat history.

Third parties

  • Hosting: [VPS provider], [database provider, e.g. Neon] in [region].
  • E-mail delivery: [SMTP provider] receives your address and the code.
  • Push notifications (Android/iOS, if enabled): Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service learn that *something* arrived for your device at a given time. They never receive message content, the sender or the conversation.

We do not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.
