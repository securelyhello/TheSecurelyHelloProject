Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Securely Hello is run by Thenn (Tirth Joshi), India. You can reach us at hello@thenn.in. This policy explains what the app and our server process, why, and for how long.

In short: we cannot read your messages, listen to your calls, or see your files and stories. They are encrypted on your phone and can only be opened by the people you send them to.

What stays on your devices

  • The content of your messages, calls, files, voice notes and stories. They are encrypted before they leave your device and decrypted only on the devices of the people you are talking to, and on your own other devices.
  • Your private keys and your chat history.
  • Your notification settings, mutes and drafts.

Who else is involved

  • Hosting: our server runs on a VPS from Manage Server in India; the database is hosted by Neon in Singapore.
  • E-mail: Resend sends verification and reset codes, so it receives your address and the code.
  • Push notifications (only if turned on): Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's push service learn that something arrived for your device and when. They never receive content, the sender or the chat.
  • Calls: when two phones cannot connect directly, our own relay server passes the call along. It stays end-to-end encrypted.
  • Updates: the Android app downloaded from our website checks securelyhello.thenn.in for new versions about twice a day. Like any website, ours sees the address the request comes from.

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Its fonts come with the app, so it makes no requests to font services. We do not sell or share your data.

Your rights

In the app you can see and change your profile, remove devices, and delete your account (Settings → Delete account), which deletes everything our server holds about you. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or anything else, write to hello@thenn.in. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Securely Hello is not meant for people under 18 without the verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian.

Security

Messages, calls, files and stories are end-to-end encrypted with the Signal protocol design (X3DH and the Double Ratchet). Connections to our server use TLS. You can compare safety numbers with a contact to be sure nobody is in between.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact and grievances

Write to hello@thenn.in. Our Grievance Officer is Tirth Joshi, at the same address. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 15 days.

Not even we can read your chats.
