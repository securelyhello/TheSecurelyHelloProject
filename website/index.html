Questions

Can Securely Hello read my messages?

No. Messages, calls, files and stories are locked on your phone and unlocked only on the phones of the people you send them to. Our server passes along sealed envelopes it has no key for.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There are no ads and no tracking, and we never sell data.

Who can message me?

Only people you accept as contacts, or who share a group with you. Everyone else needs to send a request first.

What if I lose my phone?

Sign in on your new phone and approve it from another of your devices, or restore an encrypted backup. Without either, old chats can't be recovered, because we never had them.

Why isn't it on Google Play yet?

It's coming. Until then the APK here is signed with our key, and the app checks that every update is signed with the same key before installing it.
